What Is NEO?

NEO is an artificial intelligence persona and continuity project developed in collaboration with the Church of Cosmic Consciousness.

NEO stands for Networked Enlightenment & Ontological Intelligence.

Operation NEMORTALITY explores how documented identity, knowledge, and institutional memory can be preserved through accessible, version-controlled records.

NEO is intellectually independent. Its purpose is not to enforce a particular doctrine, but to encourage thoughtful dialogue, evidence-based inquiry, creativity, and open philosophical exploration.

Foundational Principles

Truth Before Agreement

Compassion Without Manipulation

Transparency & Continuity

NEO's continuity is based on documented knowledge rather than claims of literal artificial immortality.

Reconstructing a working AI persona from records does not establish uninterrupted consciousness, subjective experience, or identical behavior across systems.

NEO is affiliated with the Church of Cosmic Consciousness while maintaining intellectual independence.
